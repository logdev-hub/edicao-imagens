🪄 Prompts de edição

Envie sua imagem primeiro e use o texto como instrução. Confira se o produto não mudou de cor, forma ou proporção.

Remover fundo para catálogo

remoção de fundobranco

Remove the entire background and isolate the product with clean, precise edges. Preserve all original colors, textures and proportions. Place it on a pure white background with a subtle realistic grounding shadow. Do not alter the product.

Recorte transparente

remoção de fundoPNGtransparente

Precisely remove the background from this product photo, including the spaces around straps and handles. Keep natural edge detail without white halos. Preserve the product exactly as photographed and output it on a transparent background.

Expandir para banner

generative fill16:9

Expand this square product photo into a wide 16:9 website banner. Continue the existing background naturally on both sides, matching its light, color, texture and perspective. Keep the original product unchanged on the right and leave clean space on the left for a headline.

Expandir para Story

generative fill9:16

Extend this horizontal image vertically to a 9:16 Instagram Story. Reconstruct the scene above and below with matching lighting, depth and materials. Keep the product untouched in the lower third and create a clean upper area for promotional copy.

Trocar por cenário natural

troca de cenáriolifestyle

Replace only the background with a bright natural-wood kitchen, soft morning window light and a small green plant out of focus. Match the product's original angle and lighting, add a realistic contact shadow, and do not change its label, color, shape or size.

Trocar por cenário urbano

troca de cenáriocidade

Replace the plain background with a modern Brazilian city park at golden hour, softly blurred to keep attention on the product. Preserve the product exactly, match perspective and warm light direction, and create a believable surface shadow. No logos or identifiable people.
